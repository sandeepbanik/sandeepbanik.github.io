Sandeep Banik

Welcome! I'm a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Naira Hovakimyan. I obtained my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Michigan State University in 2023, advised by Prof. Shaunak D. Bopardikar. Before that, I was a research assistant at IIIT-Delhi and completed my M.Sc. at KU Leuven, Belgium.

I develop game-theoretic and control-theoretic foundations for principled authority allocation among humans, robots, and AI agents: deciding when an autonomous system should act, defer, or hand over control, and with what guarantees. My work combines dynamic game theory, robust and adaptive control, and learning to build certifiable shared autonomy, adversarially resilient cyber-physical systems, and safe learning-enabled control.

Research Interests

  • Shared autonomy
  • Human–robot & human–AI teaming
  • Learning-enabled robot control
  • L1 adaptive & robust control
  • Multi-robot & multi-agent coordination
  • Dynamic & stochastic games
  • Distributionally robust learning
  • Safety & security of CPS
  • Shared Autonomy: Cooperative takeover games that allocate authority between a human and an autonomous agent under stochastic human override, and principled authority switching in human–robot teams. [C1, C2, S3]
  • Certifiable Learning-Enabled Control: L1-DS, a robust control layer for learned motion policies with provable tracking bounds on black-box robots; distributionally robust imitation learning with a priori robustness and performance guarantees; L1 adaptive state estimation. [C3, C4, C5]
  • Game-Theoretic Security of Cyber-Physical Systems: FlipDyn, a class of dynamic games for resource takeovers in dynamical systems and graphs; dynamic games with stopping states for attack-resilient motion planning; adversary-in-the-loop defense planning. [J1–J3, C8–C12]
  • Safe Aerial Autonomy & Multi-Agent Coordination: System-Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) for vision-based VTOL landing and take-off, potential-game coordination for time-critical multi-agent missions, and uncertainty-aware planning for UAVs. [C6, C7, S1]

I'm open to research discussions and collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
